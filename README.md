# Website Vendor Cost Benchmark Analysis

A data driven cost and risk comparison between solo or freelance development and professional agency delivery for a corporate website rebuild.

## What's inside

Compares three vendor proposals across cost, delivery timeline, payment structure, and technical stack, then models total cost of ownership over three years. Includes industry benchmark charts on project success rates, skill coverage requirements, and the relationship between site quality and lead conversion, sourced from public industry reports (Standish Group, McKinsey, Nielsen Norman Group, and others).

## Note on this document

Company and vendor names have been anonymized to protect confidentiality. All figures, analysis, and conclusions are unchanged from the original.
